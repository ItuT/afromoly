Layout preview · buttons do not change a game

1. Restore readable decisions

Open a deed sheet from a tile or a property list. Include owner, purchase price, rent ladder, group progress, development cost, and mortgage value. Show the complete exchange before accepting any trade.

2. Fix the responsive layout

Stack setup labels and trade cash fields on narrow screens. Keep the current move visible with two through six players. Use at least 44 × 44px touch areas as a design target; the current switches are 24px high.

3. Bring the world into the flat view

The cream 3D board and modeled taxis communicate the game’s setting. Reuse that palette and those token silhouettes in the flat board, setup, and home screen. Move the activity history out of the board’s main visual focus.

4. Protect the play session

Refresh currently loses your place; online rejoining by code rejects an already-started game. Add automatic recovery, clear connection feedback, and confirmation before a restart or destructive exit.
