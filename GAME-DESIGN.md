Nothing like **Afromoly: Johannesburg Edition** exists.

While Hasbro has an official *Monopoly: South Africa Edition*, it simply swaps Atlantic City street names for generic South African tourist spots (Kruger Park, Table Mountain, Vilakazi Street) using the exact same vintage 1930s rules. There is no dedicated commuting/taxi property-trading game built around Gauteng’s transit culture, Noord Taxi Rank, and the daily hustle.

---

### Key Legal & Branding Note

* **Name Caution:** While *Afromoly* is a clever portmanteau, Hasbro aggressively protects any variant ending in or derived from "-opoly" (they famously fought games like *Ghettopoly* and *Late for the Sky's* city lines). To make your commercial rollout watertight without legal headaches, consider names like **Afromopoly**, **Rank Hustle: Jozi**, **Mo-Gauteng**, or keeping *Afromoly* while making the typography and logo visually distinct from Hasbro’s red-and-white banner.

### Core Conceptual Architecture

**1. Board Topology & Transit Hubs**
Instead of static train stations, use the four major transit interchanges connecting Gauteng:

* **Noord Taxi Rank (MTN Taxi Rank)** — the chaotic beating heart.
* **Bree Taxi Rank (Lilian Ngoyi)**.
* **Park Station** (rail + long-distance buses).
* **Sandton Gautrain Station** (high-speed rail).

**2. Property Color Groups (Gauteng Corridors)**
Group the streets by iconic nodes across the socio-economic and cultural spectrum:

* **Brown (The Origins):** *Ferreirasdorp*, *Marshalltown*.
* **Light Blue (Soweto Landmarks):** *Vilakazi St*, *Diepkloof*, *Orlando Towers*.
* **Pink (Urban Culture & Creative Hubs):** *Braamfontein*, *Maboneng*, *Newtown*.
* **Orange (Inner City & East):** *Yeoville (Rockey St)*, *Hillbrow*, *Jeppestown*.
* **Red (The Strip / Nightlife):** *Melville (7th St)*, *Rosebank*, *Parkhurst (4th Ave)*.
* **Yellow (Commercial & Tech):** *Midrand*, *Waterfall City*, *Fourways*.
* **Green (Old Money & Power):** *Houghton*, *Hyde Park*, *Saxonwold*.
* **Dark Blue (The Elite Square):** *Sandton CBD*, *Alice Lane*.

**3. Mechanics Adapted to Commuter Realities**

* **Start / Go:** *“Month-End Payday”* — Collect R2,000.
* **Chance / Community Chest:**
* *“Kombi Hustle”* (Street events: "Driver forgot your change, pass it forward: pay R50", "Sliding-door operator discount: advance to Noord").
* *“City Power / Joburg Water”* (Infrastructure chaos: "Load reduction hits your block: pay R100 per house for inverter maintenance", "JMPD roadblock at Gillooly’s: pay fine or produce valid license").


* **Jail Alternative:** *“Sun City (Johannesburg Central Prison)”* or simply *“Pound / Impound Lot”* (if playing as taxi fleet owners).
* **Developments:** Instead of houses and hotels, build **Taxi Fleets** (Quantum / Siyaya) and **Rank Depots**, or **Spaza Shops** upgraded to **Commercial Plazas**.

### Special Mechanic Integration

* **Center Pot / Rank Queue Payout:** Whenever fines or informal tips are paid via cards (e.g., *Loadshedding Traffic Light* or *Private Security Levy*), place the cash into the center of the board. The next player to land on **Taxi Rank Queue (Free Rest - Space 20)** sweeps the accumulated pot.
* **Impound Bail:** If a player is sent to the **JMPD Impound Lot**, they can exit by:
1. Playing a *Get Out of JMPD Impound Free* card.
2. Paying a spot fine of **R5,000** before rolling on their turn.
3. Rolling doubles on any of their three subsequent turns. If they fail three times, they must pay **R5,000** and move the rolled distance.

The property economy follows a strict 8-color progression based on purchase price, with rent scaled to reward complete set ownership and development.

* **Monopoly Rule:** Owning all un-improved properties in a color set doubles the Base Rent.
* **Mortgage Value:** Fixed at 50% of the purchase price. Un-mortgaging requires paying the mortgage value plus a 10% administrative surcharge.

### The 6 Thematic Player Tokens

Cast in antique brushed zinc or die-cast pewter, each token embodies a distinct archetype of Johannesburg transit culture and urban hustle:

* **1. The Toyota Quantum Minibus:** Sculpted with a high roof, side decals, and a roof carrier. The quintessential symbol of Gauteng commuter transit.
* **2. The 5-Rand Bi-Metallic Coin:** An oversized metal R5 coin tilted on an integrated pavement base, representing rank change and the daily hustle.
* **3. The Traffic Light ("The Robot"):** A three-tier vertical street signal with a mesh base plate, representing city gridlock and intersection pointsmen.
* **4. The Car Guard's Hi-Vis Vest:** A miniature textured reflective vest folded into a standing A-frame, complete with radio holster detailing.
* **5. The Street Corner Megaphone:** The cone-style handheld loudspeaker mounted on a pedestal, used by rank marshals shouting destinations across Noord Rank.
* **6. The All-Star Chuck Sneaker:** A classic low-top sneaker, celebrating Joburg street culture and the millions who walk the pavements connecting transit links.

## AFROMOLY: THE JOHANNESBURG EDITION

### Official Rulebook & Fleet Operations Manual

---

### 1. Game Objective & Victory Conditions

**Objective:** Secure dominance across the Gauteng commuter basin by acquiring street corridors, operating transit hubs, developing commuter fleets, and running rival operators into financial insolvency.

* **Classic Elimination (Last Boss Standing):** The game ends when all opponents have declared bankruptcy. The sole remaining solvent player is crowned the **Undisputed Transit Tycoon of Gauteng**.
* **Timed Play Variant (Rush-Hour Sprint):** Players set a fixed time limit (e.g., 90 minutes). When time expires, all players tally their net worth:
1. Cash on hand.
2. Printed face value of all unmortgaged properties and transit hubs.
3. Half-price value of all mortgaged properties.
4. Full purchase price of all active Quantum Vans and Terminal Depots.


The player with the highest total net worth wins.

---

### 2. Components & Initial Setup

* 1 Game Board (40 tiles).
* 6 Die-Cast Metal Tokens (Quantum Minibus, R5 Coin, Traffic Light, Reflective Vest, Megaphone, Chuck Sneaker).
* 2 Standard Six-Sided Dice.
* 32 White Toyota Quantum Van miniatures.
* 12 Blue Terminal Depot miniatures.
* 28 Title Deed Cards (22 Streets, 4 Transit Hubs, 2 Utilities).
* 16 **Kombi Hustle** Event Cards.
* 16 **City Watch** Municipal Cards.
* Bank Currency Tray and Banknotes (R100, R500, R1,000, R2,000, R5,000, R10,000).

#### Board Preparation

1. Shuffle the **Kombi Hustle** and **City Watch** decks independently. Place them face down on their marked slots in the center of the board.
2. Select one player to act as the **Banker** (and Association Registrar). The Banker manages title deeds, houses/depots, auctions, and payouts. A player who is banking must keep their personal funds strictly separated from Bank reserves.
3. Distribute the starting capital of **R150,000** to each player as follows:
* 5 $\times$ R10,000 (R50,000)
* 10 $\times$ R5,000 (R50,000)
* 10 $\times$ R2,000 (R20,000)
* 15 $\times$ R1,000 (R15,000)
* 20 $\times$ R500 (R10,000)
* 50 $\times$ R100 (R5,000)


4. Each player selects a token and places it on **Month-End Payday (Start)**.

---

### 3. Turn Sequence & Movement Mechanics

Players roll both dice; the highest roll takes the first turn. Play proceeds clockwise.

1. **Roll & Move:** Roll both dice and advance your token clockwise around the board by the sum rolled.
2. **Resolve Tile:** Execute the action required by the tile landed on (purchase, pay rent, draw card, pay tax).
3. **Rolling Doubles:**
* If you roll doubles, resolve your turn as normal, then take another turn immediately.
* If you roll doubles **three consecutive times** in a single turn, your driver is flagged for reckless driving: end your turn immediately and go directly to the **JMPD Impound Lot** (do not resolve the third roll's destination).



---

### 4. Board Spaces & Tile Actions

#### A. Month-End Payday (Start)

Each time a player's token lands on or passes over Month-End Payday, the Bank pays that player a salary bonus of **R20,000**.

#### B. Unowned Properties, Transit Hubs, & Utilities

When landing on an unowned property:

* You have the first right to purchase it from the Bank at the printed price.
* **The Public Auction Rule:** If you decline to purchase the property at its printed list price, the Banker must immediately put it up for public auction. Any player (including the player who declined to buy it at face value) may bid. Bidding starts at **R500**, and the property is awarded to the highest cash bidder.

#### C. Owned Properties (Rent Collection)

When landing on a property owned by another player:

* You must pay the rent specified on the Title Deed card.
* **The Claim Rule:** The owner must verbally demand rent before the next player rolls the dice. If the owner fails to demand it before the subsequent dice roll, the rent is forfeited.
* If a player owns an entire color group and no buildings have been erected, rent on each undeveloped street in that set is doubled.

#### D. Transit Hubs (Bree, Park Station, Noord, Sandton Gautrain)

Rent scales based on how many Transit Hubs the owner possesses:

* 1 Hub: R2,500
* 2 Hubs: R5,000
* 3 Hubs: R10,000
* All 4 Hubs: R20,000

#### E. Municipal Utilities (City Power & Joburg Water)

* If 1 Utility is owned: Rent equals $400\times$ the dice roll that brought the tenant to that space.
* If both Utilities are owned: Rent equals $1,000\times$ the dice roll.
* If directed to a utility via a card instruction, roll the dice specifically to compute the multiplier payout.

#### F. Kombi Hustle & City Watch Spaces

Draw the top card from the matching deck. Read the text aloud, resolve its instructions immediately, and place the card at the bottom of the deck.

#### G. Tax Spaces

* **SARS Road Tax:** Pay flat **R20,000** or calculate **10% of your total net worth** to the Bank.
* **e-Toll / Gantry Clearance:** Pay flat **R10,000** to the Bank.

#### H. Taxi Rank Queue (Space 20 / Free Rest)

A neutral resting space. A player landing here suffers no penalties and collects no standard rent.

* *Jackpot House Rule (Official Variant):* All fines, card penalties, and e-toll payments are collected in the center of the board. The first player to land on Space 20 collects the accumulated pot. If the pot is empty, the Bank seeds it with R5,000.

---

### 5. Fleet Development (Quantums & Terminal Depots)

A player may develop properties only after owning **all** properties in that color group.

* **Even Build Rule:** You must build evenly across a set. You cannot place a second Quantum Van on any property in a set until every property in that set has one Quantum Van. The same rule applies when upgrading to third and fourth vans, and when downgrading/selling back.
* **Upgrading to a Terminal Depot:** Once all properties in a color group have 4 Quantum Vans, you may trade the 4 vans in to the Bank and pay the deed's development fee to place a **Terminal Depot**. Maximum 1 Depot per property.
* **Building Shortages:** When the Bank runs out of Quantum Vans (32 max) or Depots (12 max), no further developments may be purchased until other players sell units back to the Bank. If multiple players want the last available units, the Banker must auction each unit individually to the highest bidder.

---

### 6. Trading, Deals, & Mortgages

#### A. Trade Rules

* Unimproved properties, transit hubs, and utilities may be traded, sold, or swapped between players at any point during a player's turn for any agreed-upon combination of cash, other properties, or *Get Out of Impound* cards.
* **No Rent-Free Immunity:** Players cannot agree to "immunity deals" (e.g., "I won't charge you rent if you pass through Bree Rank"). Deals involving future promises or loans outside game mechanics are illegal.
* Properties cannot be traded if any property in that color set contains Quantum Vans or Depots. All developments on that color group must first be sold back to the Bank.

#### B. Mortgages

* A player may mortgage an unimproved property to the Bank at any time for its printed **Mortgage Value** (50% of the purchase price). Turn the card face down.
* While mortgaged, a property collects **zero rent**.
* **Lifting a Mortgage:** To un-mortgage, the owner must pay the Bank the mortgage value plus a **10% interest fee**.
* Mortgaged properties may be traded to other players. The new owner must immediately pay the 10% interest fee and may either lift the mortgage at once or keep it mortgaged (paying an additional 10% fee when they later lift it).

---

### 7. The JMPD Impound Lot: Rules & Detention Logic

A player is sent directly to the **JMPD Impound Lot** under three specific conditions:

1. Landing on the **Go to Impound Lot** corner tile (Space 30).
2. Drawing a card that commands: *"Go directly to JMPD Impound Lot"*.
3. Rolling doubles three consecutive times in a single turn.

*When sent to Impound, your turn ends immediately. You do not collect the R20,000 salary from Month-End Payday, regardless of your board position.*

#### Status: Just Visiting vs. Detained

* If you land on Space 10 via an ordinary roll, you are **Just Visiting**. Place your token on the outer curb of the space; you suffer no penalties and move normally next turn.
* If sent to Impound by rule or card, place your token inside the **Impounded Bay**.

### 8. Debt, Insolvency, & Bankruptcy

If you owe more money than you can raise in cash, you must immediately liquidate assets to settle the balance:

1. Sell Quantum Vans and Depots back to the Bank for **50% of their original purchase price** (following the even-build rule in reverse).
2. Mortgage unimproved properties to the Bank.

#### Declaring Bankruptcy

If your total cash and liquidated assets still cannot cover your debt, you must declare **Bankruptcy**:

* **Owed to Another Player:** You are eliminated from the game. Turn over all your mortgaged and unmortgaged properties to that creditor player. The recipient must immediately pay the Bank 10% interest on each mortgaged property received, choosing whether to lift the mortgages now or pay another 10% fee later.
* **Owed to the Bank (Taxes, Card Fines):** You are eliminated. All your developments are removed. The Bank takes all your property deeds and immediately auctions every single tile to the remaining solvent players, starting at R500 per deed. All your cash is absorbed by the Bank.

Once eliminated, pack up your token. The remaining operators continue until one tycoon controls Gauteng.
